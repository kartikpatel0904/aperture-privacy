Privacy Policy — Aperture

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Aperture (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a camera and photo-editing application for Android. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and what we do — and do not — collect. We take your privacy seriously: Aperture is designed to work entirely on your device.

Summary

  • Aperture does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or to any third party.
  • The app has no account system, no analytics, and no advertising.
  • All photos you capture or edit stay on your device. We never upload them.

Information the app accesses on your device

To function as a camera and photo editor, Aperture requests the following permissions. This access happens locally on your device only — the data is never sent to us or anyone else.

PermissionWhy it is neededLeaves your device?
Camera (CAMERA) To show the live viewfinder and capture photos. No
Photos / Media (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, and READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 12 and below) To let you open existing photos for editing and to show the in-app gallery of photos you saved with Aperture. No

Photos you take or edit are saved to your device’s standard photo storage (the Pictures/Aperture album). They remain under your control and are governed by your device’s own settings and your other apps’ access to shared media.

App settings

Your in-app preferences (such as the selected filter intensity, default aspect ratio, date-stamp toggle, and shutter-sound toggle) are stored locally on your device using Android’s standard preferences storage. They are not transmitted anywhere, and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Data we collect

None. Aperture does not collect, log, or transmit any personal or usage data. There is no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting service, no advertising identifier collection, and no network communication initiated by the app.

Data we share

None. Because we do not collect any data, we have nothing to share with third parties, and we do not sell data.

Children’s privacy

Aperture does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. The app operates entirely on-device.

Security

Because your photos and settings never leave your device, their security is governed by your device’s own protections (screen lock, encryption, and the OS permission model). We hold no copy of your data on any server.

Your choices

  • You can revoke the Camera or Photos permission at any time in your device’s Settings → Apps → Aperture → Permissions. Some features will stop working without the permission they rely on.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all app settings stored on your device.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change in a future version — for example, if a feature that uses the network or advertising is added — we will update this policy and the “Last updated” date above, and reflect the change in the app’s Google Play Data safety section before releasing that version.
